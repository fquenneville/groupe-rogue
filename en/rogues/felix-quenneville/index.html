Rogue Founder

Félix Quenneville

Propeller Motivator
"It's not the idea that counts — it's the execution."

Félix Quenneville is both strategist and builder, intervening where organizations begin to sense that their systems can no longer keep up with their ambition.

CISSP certified, with over 20 years in technology, he has developed a precise reading of the invisible structures that support — or hinder — execution. Governance, DevSecOps, artificial intelligence: he navigates these dimensions to identify what must be kept, eliminated, or rebuilt.

Founder of Corpo Sapiens, he designs architectures where automations, intelligent agents and coherent processes replace dependence on human effort. His approach is not to optimize what exists, but to redefine the mechanisms themselves.

An active builder, he simultaneously develops bilingual SaaS products for the Quebec market, with the same logic: transforming intuition and tacit expertise into exploitable, scalable systems.

He operates with one conviction: a high-performing organization does not rest on the intensity of its work, but on the quality of its design. His role is to reveal the flaws — then redesign the structure.
