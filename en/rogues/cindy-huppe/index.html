Rogue Founder

Cindy Huppé

Architect Coach
"Blind spots don't disappear when you ignore them. They grow."
"Authenticity is expensive in systems built for conformity. That's exactly why it's worth something."
"What I contribute to must be able to exist without me. Otherwise, I haven't built something — I've created a dependency."

Cindy operates in the space between what is said and what is true. She works in the cracks, where the light enters, but also where everything begins to give way.

Trained in anthropology, psychology, addiction and psychoeducation, she spent fifteen years inside blind spots — those of individuals, organizations and institutions that sense something no longer works, but can't find the words to say it.

What sets her apart isn't a method. It's a way of being: she perceives systems in their entirety before intervening in their details. She captures the unspoken, the silent resistances, the fractures no one has yet named. And she builds from there. With rigour, with coherence, with a deep conviction that integrity between what we think, what we say, and what we create is what gives weight to every gesture.

Her mission: that divergence, regardless of the trajectory from which it emerges, ceases to be something to correct — and becomes what it has always been: a driver.

Interventions

  • Support for broken paths and identity transitions
  • Reading of invisible human and organizational dynamics
  • Strategic support for atypical leaders and collectives in transformation
  • Design, creation and scripting of divergence-anchored learning structures
  • Creation of innovative strategic content: texts, tools, guides and teaching resources
  • Committed contribution to the movement of social literacy and collective ambition
