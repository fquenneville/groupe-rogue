Rogue Founder

Chrystian Guy

Motivator Coach Architect
"Divergence isn't a bug. It's the feature we spent years trying to patch."

Chrystian Guy operates at the intersection of the geek world and the world of organizations — not as a translator, but as a living bridge between two cultures that rarely speak to each other without causing damage.

Pioneer of Quebec's digital landscape, co-creator of La Toile du Québec in 1995 and recognized by the Internet Society of Canada in 2017, he has spent thirty years observing what organizations do with their atypical profiles: isolate them, over-invest in them, or lose them.

Founder of the X-Minds Community (x-minds.community) and architect of the ROGUE ecosystem, he embodies what he teaches. His professional identity — the Geek Whisperer — is not a marketing position. It's an operational reality: he enters organizations where leaders no longer understand their experts, and where experts have stopped trying to be understood.

His method: naming what is invisible, making what is marginalized legitimate, and building conditions where divergence becomes infrastructure — not a tolerated exception.

Interventions

  • The Geek Whisperer conferences and workshops: digital transformation, AI, bridging leaders and creative experts
  • Coaching for atypical leaders and support for neurodivergent talent
  • Co-creation of the ROGUE ecosystem: La Forge, D&L, Corpo Sapiens
  • Design and facilitation of team-building workshops for divergent teams
  • Support for public speaking for introverted leaders
