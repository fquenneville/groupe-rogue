Jenica Poirier

Architect Motivator Coach
"Ambition: Moral. Anchor: Family. Method: Collective leadership.
Target: An obsolete society ready for a divergent revolution."

Visionary, multi-entrepreneur and "Chaotic Good" deployer, Jenica Poirier merges 15 years of expertise in psychosocial intervention and management to deconstruct traditional corporatism in favour of raw, realistic, Viking-inspired leadership. A psychology graduate and mother of four krakens, this "slasher" forged by geek culture transforms divergence [whether neurocognitive, social or professional] into an engine of collective ambition. Her mission: propelling social literacy and structuring ecosystems where atypical profiles stop being marginal and become an organized force of impact, capable of durably transforming systems.

Interventions & Impact

  • Leadership training spaces for emerging leaders
  • Youth and mental health psychosocial intervention
  • Governance and management contributions across 10+ organizations
  • 1 non-profit founded
  • 3 active companies in social innovation (local and provincial)
