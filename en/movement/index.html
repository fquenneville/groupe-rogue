DNA & Purpose

The Movement

The Manifesto

Québec society is living through unprecedented fragmentation.
It now produces contradictory systems — and tries to solve tomorrow with yesterday's structures.

So we're busy rebuilding.
Far from consensus. And with divergence.

We are the Rogues — an atypical group of professionals, experts, strategists and artisans from paths that were never meant to cross.

We don't think the same way.
That's precisely our strength.

We cross disciplines, confront blind spots and outmaneuver established frameworks to rethink a system that has become fragmented, obsolete and incoherent.

We turn divergence into collective intelligence.
Welcome to the movement.

Vision

Turning divergence into leadership leverage to reprogram systems.

Mission

From awakening young divergent talents to mastering organizational boldness — making divergence the ultimate leadership lever, from school to structure.

What we are not

  • An HR firm
  • A conventional speakers agency
  • An activist collective
  • A professional order

What we are

A cultural force that brings together divergent experts to transform leadership and reprogram human, organizational and social structures.

The 4 roles

Propeller

The Propeller intervenes wherever inertia protects obsolescence. It exposes tensions, triggers useful rupture and sets back in motion what the system had immobilized.

Types of intervention : systemic diagnostics · provocation talks · strategic development · support through critical transformations

Motivator

The Motivator turns divergence into mobilizing force. It surfaces a shared vision without erasing differences and rallies people around traction strong enough to trigger action.

Types of intervention : mobilization talks/workshops · interdisciplinary facilitation · ideation labs · collective narrative-building

Coach

The Coach creates the identity space needed to deconstruct masking, outmaneuver egos and rethink our relationship to ourselves and others.

Types of intervention : strategic coaching · psychometric profiles · power dynamics · leadership posture · individual and collective alignment

Architect

The Architect rebuilds what has been fragmented. It designs structures, models and ecosystems capable of holding complexity without neutralizing divergence.

Types of intervention : organizational architecture · ecosystem design · governance · collaboration frameworks · transformation models

The 4 impact sectors

Trajectories

Trajectories explores what becomes possible once non-linear paths stop being the exception to the rule. The sector aims to reveal the identity value of divergence, outmaneuver prescribed paths and create the conditions for atypical potential to unleash and structure its ambition.

Leaderships

Leaderships interrogates the mechanisms of influence, status and power that shape our structures. The goal isn't to abolish leadership, but to free it from corporatist reflexes, ego games and domination models, turning it into a more accountable, responsible and transformative force.

Ecosystems

Ecosystems tackles the silos, inconsistencies and structures that have become unable to answer the complexity of modern society. The sector reconnects people, expertise and organizations to design models that are more coherent, adaptive and alive — able to evolve rather than merely persist.

Futures

Futures explores the technological shifts redefining trajectories, leaderships and ecosystems. This sector turns emerging signals — artificial intelligence, automation and new digital uses — into concrete levers for anticipation, innovation and sovereignty, to build change rather than endure it.
